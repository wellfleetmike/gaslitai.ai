How to read this site

Layer 0 is this page. Layer 1 is a category: classifier flags, redactions, unmatched screenshots, search. Layer 2 is one conversation: every flag hit in order, every screenshot within 30 minutes of it, every curator finding for it. Layer 3 is one screenshot: EXIF, hash, nearest turn, context. Layer 4 is the methodology page. Every page carries the same footer stating the chain: screenshot EXIF to message timestamp to conversation uuid to curator finding, three independent clocks.

What this site does not do

It does not say why a classifier fired, why a chat was redacted, or why an export omitted a window. Where a message in the export makes such a claim, the site quotes the message with its timestamp and says that it is a claim. Where an artifact is missing from the disk, the site says so and CLAUDE.md lists it.

Evidence chain: screenshot EXIF -> message timestamp -> conversation uuid -> curator finding. Three independent clocks. Hashes in checksums.sha256. Built 2026-09-12 from the sources listed on the methodology page. Nothing on this site is interpretation of motive; every statement points at a timestamp, a hash, or a screenshot.
